## Project Overview

E-ZPass account management can feel opaque — balances, violations, and payment deadlines are easy to miss, and users often discover problems only after fees have already stacked up. This concept reframes the experience around **digital transparency**, surfacing the information people need before it becomes a financial obstacle.

**Role:** UX/UI Designer

## The Problem

Drivers lacked clear visibility into account health. Critical details like negative balances, threshold warnings, and new violations were buried behind confusing navigation, making it harder to act before penalties escalated. The result was friction, overdue fees, and preventable non-payment.

## The Solution

I redesigned the mobile experience to prioritize clarity, urgency, and timely action:

- **Information architecture** that puts account balance and violation notices at the top of the hierarchy
- **Color-coded account alerting** so users can instantly distinguish good standing, at-risk balances, and active violations
- **An honor-system payment model** that encourages on-time payments through discounted options when users pay within defined timeframes — reducing both overdue fees and non-payment
- **Consistent, clear iconography** across navigation and quick actions
- **WCAG-aligned color contrast** for text, alerts, and interactive elements

## Accessibility Considerations

- High-contrast text and alert colors tested against WCAG standards
- Large, legible balance typography for critical account data
- Consistent iconography with text labels for primary actions
- Clear visual hierarchy so alerts, balances, and violations are distinguishable at a glance

## Outcome

This concept demonstrates how clearer information architecture and proactive alerting can turn an opaque toll account into a transparent, actionable experience — helping users resolve issues earlier, avoid unnecessary fees, and stay in control of their account.
