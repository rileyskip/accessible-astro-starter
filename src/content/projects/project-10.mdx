## Project Overview

E-ZPass account management can be difficult to understand. Balances, violations, and payment deadlines are easy to miss. Users often learn about problems only after fees have increased.

This concept focuses on digital transparency. It presents account information clearly and gives users time to act before problems become more costly.

**Role:** UX/UI Design Concept

## The Problem

Drivers did not have clear visibility into account status. Negative balances, threshold warnings, and violations were hard to find. This made it more difficult to respond before penalties increased.

## The Solution

The mobile interface prioritizes clear status, timely alerts, and direct actions:

- Account balance and violation notices appear at the top of the screen hierarchy.
- Color-coded alerts help users distinguish good standing, at-risk balances, and active violations.
- An honor-system payment model offers reduced fees when users pay within set timeframes.
- Navigation and quick actions use consistent iconography.
- Text, alerts, and interactive elements follow WCAG color contrast guidelines.

## Accessibility Considerations

- Text and alert colors were tested against WCAG standards.
- Balance amounts use large, readable type.
- Primary actions include text labels in addition to icons.
- Alerts, balances, and violations use distinct visual hierarchy.

## Outcome

This concept shows how clearer information structure and timely alerts can make toll account management easier to understand. Users can respond to problems earlier, reduce unnecessary fees, and stay informed about account status.
